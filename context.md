# Project Context

## Goal
Build a front-end only portal prototype that can be used in sales conversations with plumbing and landscaping companies. The product should demonstrate practical AI value in dispatch, intake, and revenue operations.

## Market Research Snapshot
Research signals used for positioning:
- Plumbing operations face labor pressure and dispatch complexity; software buyers prioritize assigning the best available technician quickly.
- Home service buyers increasingly expect online scheduling, rapid communication, and transparent ETA updates.
- Revenue growth in field services is tied to faster quote turnaround, reduced repeat visits, and better follow-up automation.
- Landscaping businesses are highly sensitive to route density, seasonality, and recurring maintenance contract retention.
- Operational dashboards that connect crew execution to revenue outcomes are a common buying trigger for owner-led teams.

Primary source used in this project:
- ServiceTitan industry and operations content focused on plumbing performance benchmarks, customer expectations, dispatch automation, and reporting workflows.

## Sales Positioning
### Primary Pain Points
- Slow intake-to-dispatch handoff.
- Underutilized technicians/crews and inefficient routing.
- Missed revenue from poor upsell timing and delayed estimates.
- Limited visibility into SLA risk and job profitability.

### Demo Narrative
1. Show AI intake converting raw requests into structured work orders.
2. Show AI dispatch balancing skill-match, route time, and job value.
3. Show AI-generated recommendations that connect operations to revenue impact.
4. Show owner-level outcomes: utilization, first-time fix, SLA risk, and ticket growth.

### Business Outcomes to Emphasize
- Faster response and reduced missed appointments.
- Better labor utilization and route efficiency.
- Improved close rates via smarter quote prompts.
- Stronger retention for recurring service agreements.

## Scope
- Front-end only.
- Vite + React + Tailwind.
- Includes AI affordances, loading states, and interactive commands suitable for live demo pitching.
