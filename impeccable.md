# Design Context

## Product
AI-powered internal operations portal demo for plumbing and landscaping companies.

## Audience
- Owner-operators who need stronger revenue control and less scheduling chaos.
- Dispatch and office coordinators managing same-day work and crew allocation.
- Field supervisors who need clear visibility into route load, SLA risk, and team productivity.

## Visual Direction
- Dark technical and credible, not playful and not futuristic hype.
- Cyan is the primary accent and purple usage is avoided.
- Editorial dashboard feel: clear rails, strong hierarchy, restrained highlights.
- No gradient typography.

## UX Principles
- Prioritize job throughput clarity over decorative UI.
- Surface AI as practical copilot actions, not abstract claims.
- Always show business impact next to operational data (margin, utilization, close rate).
- Keep key interactions within one screen for demo flow speed.

## Motion
- Purposeful loading and scanning animations to communicate AI processing.
- Motion must be subtle, performant, and respect reduced-motion settings.

## Responsive
- Full portal behavior on desktop and tablet.
- Mobile preserves core actions (vertical switch, AI command, active job stream) without hiding critical data.
